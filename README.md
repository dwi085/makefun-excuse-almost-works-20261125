## The task

Write the single line a gamer texts the boss to get out of work during
GTA 6 launch week. One line, plain text, at most 20 words, reading like a
real hurried text — judged by a tired manager who has heard every excuse
twice and believes almost none of them.

## The excuse

> boss sorry can't come in, my boiler burst overnight and soaked my gta 6 preorder, plumber's only free this morning

20 words. A boring household disaster, a launch-week detail baked in, and a
time-boxed reason to stay home. No sick-day cliché, no dead grandmother, no
flat tyre, no sudden fever.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
